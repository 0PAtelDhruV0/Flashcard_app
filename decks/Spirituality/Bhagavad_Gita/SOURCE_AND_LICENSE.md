# Bhagavad Gita — Adhyaya 18 · Source and attribution

This Deckflow deck contains all 78 verses of Bhagavad Gita chapter 18 (मोक्षसंन्यासयोगः).

- **Sanskrit Devanagari and romanization source:** [AdiyogiArts/gita-json](https://github.com/AdiyogiArts/gita-json), `base.json`, © 2024 Adiyogi Arts; MIT licensed.
- **English meanings:** Short original educational paraphrases prepared for this flashcard deck, not a substitute for a full scholarly translation or commentary.
- **Speech:** `hi-IN` provides a Hindi-language browser-voice fallback for Devanagari; it does not ensure classical Sanskrit pronunciation.
- **Additional text comparison:** [Kosha Sanskrit Gita, Chapter 18](https://kosha.sanskrit.today/b/bhagavad-gita/s/ch-18).
- **Deck format:** `front,back,note,level,type,audio_lang`; the `front` has the Sanskrit shloka, `back` has its English meaning, `note` has a romanized reading aid.
