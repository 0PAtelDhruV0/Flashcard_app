Parlez GitHub deck pack
=======================

Repository: https://github.com/0PAtelDhruV0/Flashcard_app

1. Open repository; on main, choose Add file > Upload files.
2. Upload the individual 14 French_*_Deck.csv files from this ZIP.
3. Do not upload French_All_New_Decks.csv (it is a backup, and the app ignores all-decks filenames).
4. Commit changes to main.
5. Open https://0pateldhruv0.github.io/Flashcard_app/ and tap Refresh decks.

Your existing index.html should remain unchanged. The app discovers new CSV files automatically.
